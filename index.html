Share of voice in AI answers

Tracked 124 non-branded prompts across 11 markets and 5 AI models (2,480 responses). The brand's own domain is the most-cited source in the category, ahead of YouTube and social media, and about 9 in 10 brand mentions are positive.

Mentions and citations rose in all 11 markets month over month, while organic clicks fell. Part of that drop came from seasonal holiday pages and a Google Search Console impressions bug, so the channel mix shifted rather than relevance dropping.
